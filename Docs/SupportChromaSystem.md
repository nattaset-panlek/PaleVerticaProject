# Support Skill Chroma & Mineral System

Support Skills in **Realm of Hero** are categorized by their **Chroma** (Type/Color) and **Mineral** subclass to determine their compatibility, flavor, and base mechanical behavior.

## Chroma & Mineral Classification

### 1. กายภาพ (Physical)

Reflects physical force, strength, impact, and tangible structures.

* **Hematite (เฮมาไทต์)**: Red iron ore with high density, representing raw power, durability, and physical modifications.
* **Obsidian (ออบซิเดียน)**: Sharp volcanic glass, representing weapons, critical sharpness, and armor-penetrating physical strikes.

### 2. เวท (Magic / Arcane)

Reflects pure spellcasting energy, elemental transformations, and alchemy.

* **Fluorite (ฟลูออไรต์)**: Fluorescent mineral containing multiple colors, representing energy shifting and elemental conversions.
* **Labradorite (ลาบราโดไรต์)**: The wizard's stone, reflecting deep gold-blue light, representing spell amplification and magical enhancements.

### 3. จิตวิญญาณ (Soul / Spirit)

Reflects abstract forces, life/mana essence, and status modifications.

* **Celestite (เซเลสไทต์)**: Pale blue sky crystal, representing spiritual serenity, mana regeneration, and status enhancements.
* **Moldavite (โมลดาไวต์)**: High-frequency meteorite glass, representing lifeforce leeching, soul manipulation, and raw critical amplification.

### 4. นอกดารา (Cosmic / Stellar)

Reflects forces originating from deep space, space-time constants, and stellar gravity.

* **Tektite (เทกไทต์)**: Rough black cosmic glass, representing projectile speed, range extension, and orbital duration.
* **Pallasite (พัลลาไซต์)**: Beautiful olivine-iron meteorite, representing projectile splitting, gravitational pull, and cosmic damage scaling.

### 5. ต่างมิติ (Dimensional / Void)

Reflects dimensional hopping, void manipulation, and space bending.

* **Alexandrite (เจ้าสามสี)**: Color-shifting crystal, representing cooldown reduction, projectile homing, and transition between states.
* **Ulexite (ทีวีสโตน)**: Fiber-optic TV stone, representing mana cost bending, teleportation, and portal-like dimensional redirection.

### 6. ต้นกำเนิด (Origin / Primordial)

Reflects eternal elements, primordial creation, and fundamental states.

* **Zircon (เซอร์คอน / เพทาย)**: The oldest mineral on Earth, representing base damage multiplication and eternal status resistance.
* **Amber (อำพัน)**: Ancient fossilized resin preserving life, representing elemental damage conversion (e.g. burn/freeze) and fundamental status effects.
